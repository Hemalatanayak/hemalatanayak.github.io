About Me

I am a PhD student in Data Science at the College of William & Mary, with an M.Sc. in Physics from Central University of Karnataka. My research interests lie in the intersection of physics and data science, with a focus on cutting-edge technologies like Graph Neural Networks and Large Language Models for complex data analysis.

My Research Projects

Characterization of Silicon Photomultiplier (SiPM)

Conducted rigorous characterization of cutting-edge Silicon Photomultipliers (SiPMs) to determine parameters like photon detection efficiency, dark count rate, and time resolution. This work ensures reliable and accurate performance for applications in medical imaging and high-energy physics.

GNN-based energy reconstruction for INO-ICAL

Developed a Graph Neural Network (GNN) based approach for neutrino energy reconstruction to verify its efficiency and speed compared to conventional techniques. This research was presented at an international conference.
